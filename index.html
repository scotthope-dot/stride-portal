Data Controller & Statutory Registration: Personal data is collected and processed by Stride Together CIC, Company Registration No: 17243958, Registered Address: 33 Crabble Hill, Dover, Kent, CT17 0RX. Stride Together CIC is registered as a Data Controller with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO Registration Certificate - ZC237149).

• Purpose & Legal Basis: Personal and health information is processed solely to assess physical readiness, deliver safe session coaching, contact emergency services if required, and provide anonymised, aggregated demographic reports to funders. Medical data is classified as Special Category Data under Article 6(1)(a) and Article 9(2)(a) of the UK GDPR.
• Storage & Security: Your data is stored securely in password-protected, encrypted Google Workspace shared drives accessible only by authorized directors, coaches, and first aiders.
• Data Retention: PAR-Q health declarations are valid for 12 months and retained for 3 years post-inactivity in compliance with sports liability insurance audit requirements, after which records are securely destroyed.
• Your Legal Rights: Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request erasure of your data, or withdraw consent at any time by contacting stridetogethercic@gmail.com.
